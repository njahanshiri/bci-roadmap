# Part 09: Research, ethics, and community

**When:** all 5 years, starting in week 1.

## Reading and research
- [ ] How to read a BCI paper: methods first, and check how they split the data
- [ ] Reproducing a paper and documenting where your results differ
- [ ] Keeping up: *J Neural Eng*, *IEEE TNSRE*, *IEEE TBME*, arXiv and bioRxiv
- [ ] Writing a preprint; choosing venues (BCI Society Meeting, Graz BCI Conference, IEEE EMBC, NeurIPS workshops)
- [ ] Peer review: reviewing other people's work

## Open science
- [ ] Reproducible code: pinned environments, fixed seeds, one-command runs
- [ ] Sharing data in BIDS; licenses for code and data
- [ ] Preregistration and reporting negative results

## Ethics
- [ ] Informed consent and ethics board (IRB) approval
- [ ] Neural data privacy and neurorights
- [ ] Hype vs. evidence: communicating results honestly
- [ ] Accessibility, and involving end users with disabilities

## Community
- [ ] Join NeuroTechX and the OpenBCI forum
- [ ] First contribution to MNE, MOABB, Braindecode, or pyRiemann
- [ ] Publish one post for each finished notebook
- [ ] Join a hackathon (e.g. NeuroTechX or BCI Society hackathons)
- [ ] Give a talk or run a study group
- [ ] Mentor someone who is just starting
